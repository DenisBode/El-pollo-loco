Class: SoundManager

SoundManager()

Plays the background music and all sound effects; mute switches everything off at once. The sounds are loaded completely into memory first (with new tries if the connection is bad) and played from there with the Web Audio API, so they play at once, overlap each other and do not depend on the network any more.
