Class: Endboss

Endboss()

The endboss: waits until he is seen and shows an alert. Then he fights: he walks up to Pepe, charges at him, backs off, rests, and sometimes jumps. The less energy he has, the faster and angrier he gets. After his death he tumbles and comes to rest on the ground.

Constructor

new Endboss()

Creates the endboss at the end of the level; he is far away, so his images load after the start images.
